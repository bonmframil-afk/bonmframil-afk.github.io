View Reports Explore GIS

THE IDEA

Communication meets location.

The telegraph made it possible to transmit information quickly across long distances. Bayanihan Alert reimagines this idea for modern communities by combining information sharing with GIS.

01

Telegraph

The historical foundation. The telegraph allowed information to travel quickly between places.

02

Communication

Community members can communicate information about problems they notice in their surroundings.

03

GIS

Geographic information adds location and context, helping people understand where an incident is happening.

HOW IT WORKS

From noticing to knowing.

01

Notice

Notice a problem or situation affecting your community.

02

Report

Share the problem with its category, location, date, and description.

03

Map

The information can be connected to a location and visualized through GIS.

04

Confirm

Other community members can confirm information they have personally witnessed.

05

Stay Informed

Check the GIS map and official news to understand what is happening and where it is happening.
